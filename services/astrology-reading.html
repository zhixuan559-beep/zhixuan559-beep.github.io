Astrology FAQ

How much does an astrology chart reading cost?

Readings start at $30. The exact price depends on the depth you want — a core natal chart reading or a fuller reading with more areas covered — and it is confirmed by email before you pay, so the price is always fixed in advance.

What information do you need for my chart?

Your birth date, your birth time, and your birth city. The birth time sets your rising sign and house placements, so the more exact it is, the more precise the reading. If you do not know your birth time, say so — the reading can still cover your Sun, Moon, and planetary signs, and will note which parts need a birth time.

What does the reading cover?

Your Sun, Moon, and rising sign; where each planet sits by sign and house; and the major aspects between planets. In plain terms: personality and emotional needs, how you work and communicate, relationship patterns, career direction, and the tensions and strengths that run through the chart as a whole.

How and when do I receive the reading?

The reading is a written document delivered by email within 1–3 days. There is no call or live session — you receive a complete report you can keep and re-read.

Can I ask about one specific area, like career or relationships?

Yes. Mention the area in your email and the reading will go deeper there while still covering the chart as a whole, so the focused section sits in context.
