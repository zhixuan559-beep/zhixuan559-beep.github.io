Contract Review

Know what you are signing — before you sign it.

Most contract trouble hides in clauses that look like boilerplate: unlimited liability, IP you did not mean to give away, a non-compete wider than the job. This service reads your contract clause by clause, flags every term that exposes you, and explains it in plain English — with wording you can push back with.

Order by email Order on Fiverr

Risks this review looks for

  • Liability — uncapped or one-sided liability and indemnity clauses
  • Intellectual property — assignments broader than the work you were hired for
  • Termination — who can walk away, when, and what you are still owed
  • Payment — late-payment gaps, kill fees, expenses left undefined
  • Non-compete & exclusivity — restrictions wider or longer than reasonable
  • Confidentiality — one-way NDAs and terms that survive too long
  • Auto-renewal & lock-in — quiet renewals and hard exits
  • Disputes — governing law and venue set somewhere that disadvantages you

Contract Review FAQ

How much does a contract review cost?

Three fixed tiers: the Risk Scan is $50 for a short contract and flags the clauses that expose you; the Full Review is $100 and goes clause by clause with a plain-English explanation of each risk and suggested alternative wording; the $150 tier covers longer or more complex contracts and adds redline-style suggested edits plus a priority list of what to negotiate first. Prices are in USD and confirmed by email before you pay.

How fast is the review delivered?

Within 2 days. The review is delivered as a written report by email, organized clause by clause so you can work through it with the other party.

What kinds of contracts can you review?

Freelance and contractor agreements, client service contracts, NDAs, licensing and IP terms, and small-business agreements — the contracts freelancers and small businesses sign every week. If your contract is outside that scope, you will be told honestly before paying anything.

What exactly do I get back?

A written report that goes through your contract clause by clause: each risky term quoted, what it means in plain English, why it is risky for you, and — on the $100 and $150 tiers — suggested wording you can propose instead. Typical risks flagged include unlimited liability, one-sided termination, broad IP assignment, non-competes, late-payment gaps, and auto-renewal traps.

Is this legal advice?

No. This is a contract risk-spotting and explanation service, not a law firm and not legal advice. It helps you understand what you are signing and negotiate from knowledge. For high-value or disputed matters, take the report to a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction.
