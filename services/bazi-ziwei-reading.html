BaZi + Zi Wei FAQ

What is a BaZi and Zi Wei chart reading?

BaZi (Four Pillars of Destiny) maps the year, month, day, and hour of your birth into Chinese five-element terms to describe your character, strengths, and life cycles. Zi Wei Dou Shu plots stars across twelve life palaces — career, wealth, relationships, family, and more. This service reads both charts together and delivers the result as a long-form written report in plain English.

How much does a BaZi + Zi Wei reading cost?

Three fixed tiers: the Overview is $30 and covers your core chart structure and main life themes; the Full Reading is $100 and covers both charts section by section, including career, wealth, relationships, and 10-year luck cycles; the $150 tier adds a deeper combined cross-check of both charts plus follow-up questions by email. Prices are in USD and confirmed before you pay.

What information do you need for my reading?

Your birth date, your birth time as exactly as you know it, your birth city, and your gender. Birth time matters because it sets the hour pillar in BaZi and the palace layout in Zi Wei; if you do not know your exact birth time, say so in your email and the reading will note which parts are affected.

How and when do I receive the reading?

The reading is a written document delivered by email within 1–3 days depending on the tier. There is no call or live session — you receive a complete report you can keep and re-read.

What topics does the reading cover?

The Full Reading covers personality and Day Master strength, career direction, wealth patterns, relationships and marriage, family, health tendencies, and the timing of your 10-year luck cycles and current year. If one area matters most to you, mention it in your email and the reading will go deeper there.
