A taste of the work: EMBERWYN

From a written sample prepared for a fictional small-batch candle studio in Portland — shown here in brief to demonstrate the depth of a full delivery.

EMBERWYN (say it: EM-ber-win) joins ember — the low, steady glow after the flame — with wyn, from Welsh gwyn, “fair, blessed.” Warmth first, calm second: a fire you sit beside, not a barbecue brand. It reads cleanly on a jar label, works as a social handle, and was spot-checked against live candle brands — the obvious rivals in that field were already taken, which is exactly why a coined name like this is worth having. Naming FAQ

How much does the naming service cost?

Three fixed tiers: Starter is $30 for 5 original name ideas with meaning and origin notes; Standard is $100 for 10 name ideas with full pronunciation, origin, and layered-meaning breakdowns; Premium is $150 and adds tagline options for the top 3 plus social handle and domain availability notes. Prices are in USD and confirmed by email before you pay.

How long does a naming order take?

Delivery is 1–3 days depending on the tier. The names arrive as a written document by email, so you can compare them side by side and share them with a partner or co-founder.

What information do you need from me to start?

For a brand name: what the business or product is, who it is for, the feeling the name should give, and any words or styles to avoid. For a baby name: the family name, names you already like or dislike, and any meaning, language, or cultural roots you want included. A few sentences by email is enough.

Do you check whether the names are already taken?

Every shortlist is spot-checked against live brands in the same field, and names that collide with a real business are dropped. The Premium tier also includes social handle and domain availability notes. A formal trademark search with an attorney is still recommended before printing labels or filing registrations.

Can you name a baby as well as a business?

Yes — the same service covers both. Baby name lists focus on meaning, sound with the family name, and how the name reads and ages; brand name lists focus on memorability, ownability, and how the name works on a label, a handle, and a sign.
