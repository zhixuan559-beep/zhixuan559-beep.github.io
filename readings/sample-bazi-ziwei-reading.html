Part 1 — The BaZi reading (Four Pillars)

Orientation — how to use this reading

This report reads your birth chart the way a BaZi practitioner works through it: first the chart itself, then what the chart says, then what it does not say. BaZi is a map of tendencies, not a script. Where the evidence in the chart is strong, I say so. Where it is genuinely ambiguous, I mark it unknown rather than guess — a reading that admits its limits is worth more than one that pretends to know everything.

Nothing here is a medical, legal or financial instruction. Treat the action notes at the end as reflection prompts, not commands.

Step 2 — The Day Master: Jia Wood

The Day Stem is you in a BaZi chart. Yours is Jia Wood — the tall tree. Jia people grow in one direction: upward. The classic image is a pine that would rather crack than bend, which in modern language looks like this: you do best with a clear direction and room to grow, and worst inside structures that demand constant small compromises. This is the single most repeated theme in your chart, and it returns in the career and relationship sections below.

Step 3 — Strength of the Day Master

You were born in Mao month — mid-spring, when Wood is at its seasonal peak. Your Day Master also sits on Shen, which cuts the other way: Shen carries Metal, the element that prunes Wood.

Verdict: strong, but pruned. You are not a weak chart that needs propping up; you are a strong tree growing through a metal gate. In practice this tends to show up as real ability paired with a feeling of being supervised, graded or constrained — often by systems (employers, institutions, family expectations) rather than by any one person.

Step 4 — The Ten Gods: what surrounds you

  • Two Xin Metal stems (Year and Month) — the Officer stars. Authority, rules, reputation. Doubled, this is a chart that cares about doing things properly and being seen to do so. It also means pressure from standards is a lifelong companion, not a phase.
  • Ji Earth in the Hour pillar — the Wealth star. Earth is what Wood controls, and in the Hour position it speaks about later-life results: money and tangible outcomes arrive more reliably in the second half of life than the first.
  • Si Fire in the Hour branch — the Output star. Fire is Wood's expression: teaching, explaining, making, performing. It sits quietly under the Wealth star, which produces the classic pattern output generates wealth — you earn by what you express and produce, not by speculation.

Step 5 — Favourable and unfavourable elements

  • Favourable: Water (nourishes Wood, softens Metal's pressure — study, rest, mentors, reflection) and Fire (your output — creating and communicating is how this chart converts strength into results).
  • Use with care: Metal. Some Metal gives you shape and standards; too much turns into self-criticism and rigid environments.
  • Unknown: whether Earth (Wealth) is favourable depends on the luck cycle in force — see Step 6. In your current cycle it is workable; in the previous one it mostly meant over-responsibility.

Step 6 — Luck pillars and the current decade

Your luck pillars run in ten-year themes. The current cycle (roughly ages 34–44) shifts the chart's emphasis from being evaluated to producing and owning. Clients with this pattern often describe the same turning point in plain words: "I stopped waiting to be picked." The chart supports that reading — this is the decade where the Output-to-Wealth pattern in your Hour pillar becomes the main road, and where Water-type activities (learning a new field, writing, building an audience) stop being hobbies and start being infrastructure.

The next two years inside this cycle carry extra Fire — favourable for visibility and launching work, less favourable for rest. Plan recovery the way you plan work.

Chapter summary — the three themes that repeat

  1. Grow upward, don't spread sideways. Every strong signal in this chart points the same way: depth in one direction beats breadth.
  2. Standards are your engine and your cage. The doubled Officer stars make you excellent and exhausted by the same mechanism.
  3. Your second half out-earns your first. The chart's wealth pattern is back-loaded. Early slowness is structural, not a verdict on you.

Career & resources

Best fit: roles where expertise is visible and judged on output — specialist, consultant, teacher, maker, founder-of-one. Worst fit: large hierarchies where advancement depends on patience with process. Income pattern: expression first, money second, in that order; reversing the order stalls both.

Relationships

Jia Wood partners loyally and argues about principles rather than feelings, which partners can experience as stubbornness. The chart's Officer weight means you respect a partner who has their own standards. Unknown: the chart shows your pattern in relationships, not any specific person's behaviour — it cannot tell you what a partner will do.

Part 2 — The Zi Wei Dou Shu reading

1. How to read this report

Zi Wei Dou Shu identifies the person first, and talks about timing second: the natal chart is the map, the decade cycles and annual cycles are the weather. The reading order is fixed — find the Life Palace, then the Body Palace, then read the Life Palace's triangle (Wealth, Career, Travel), then the year's Four Transformations, and only then the decade and annual cycles. The order itself is the credibility: who you are, clearly stated, before what these years will bring.

This is the sample layer: the chart's structure, main axis, triangle and this year's focus are all here, properly formed. What it does not do is open all twelve palaces one by one, or give the breakthrough playbook in concrete steps — those are the paid layer. If you finish this feeling you want to ask further, the further questions are exactly what the paid reading answers.

Where the evidence is insufficient, I write unknown rather than guess. A reading that admits its limits is worth more than one that pretends to know everything.

3. Life Palace: Zi Wei and Tian Fu together in Yin

Your Life Palace holds Zi Wei, the Emperor star, and Tian Fu, the Treasury star, side by side in Yin — one of the most well-formed combinations in a Zi Wei chart: a natural head-of-the-table quality. Not the finger-pointing kind of in-charge — the kind where, when something lands in your hands, you instinctively gather it up, finish it, and take responsibility for it to the end.

Zi Wei is transformed to Ke (Fame) by your birth year (see Section 6): reputation is not decoration for you, it is an asset. People remember you for something you did beautifully, not for something you said. This transformation also means your credit is your passport — protected, the road keeps widening; overdrawn, it is expensive to rebuild.

But a Zi Wei–Tian Fu chart has a known habit: many ledgers inside, a steady face outside. Other people see composure; in fact you are often running three sets of accounts in your head at once. That is not hypocrisy — it is this chart's factory setting. The paid layer opens those three ledgers one by one.

4. Body Palace in Wealth: where life applies more force as you age

The Body Palace sits in your Wealth Palace, with Tian Xiang presiding. It means: when young you may want to try everything, but as the years pass, life converges on one place — money, and making things that are worth money.

Tian Xiang carries the temperament of a seal-keeper: propriety, standards, things presented properly. With it ruling Wealth, your money does not come from speculation; it comes from building a reputation and then converting reputation into income. Anxiety about money when young is mostly wasted anxiety with this chart — it is not an early-glory chart, it is a compound-interest-of-work chart: what you stack up in the first half gets settled, all together, in the second.

Unknown: Tian Xiang wealth fears one thing — sloppy speed. Whether the venture currently in your hands should go fast or go steady depends on the annual cycles inside your current decade; the sample layer does not open that. The paid layer goes year by year.

5. The triangle: Life, Wealth, Career, Travel read together

Reading the Life Palace alone flattens a person. The four points are read as one:

  • Wealth Palace · Tian Xiang (Body Palace here): as above — money through propriety and reputation.
  • Career Palace · Lian Zhen, alone: the career star is Lian Zhen, a star both upright and unruly: upright, it is talent, charisma, and the nerve to attempt things; astray, it is entanglement — fighting yourself. Your career question is never "will it go smoothly?" It is "is this worth fighting myself over?" Pick the right arena and Lian Zhen is your engine; pick the wrong one and it is the source of your exhaustion.
  • Travel Palace · Qi Sha, alone: leaving, relocating, changing environments scores points for you. Qi Sha in Travel means you are stronger in motion than at rest: business trips, moving cities, taking work from overseas clients online — the chart supports all of it. Staying perfectly still is what actually drains you.
  • The three together: Life Palace says "run your own table," Career says "fight," Travel says "move." This is not a chart for climbing someone else's ladder by seniority. It is a chart for building your own stage and standing on it — which matches the road you are already on.

6. The Four Transformations of the birth year: your smoothest square, and the one you cannot let go of

The birth year stamps four kinds of force onto the chart; where each lands, there is a story. (Yi year: Tian Ji → Lu, Tian Liang → Quan, Zi Wei → Ke, Tai Yin → Ji.)

  • Tian Ji transformed to Lu (Wealth-in-flow) in the Parents Palace: elders, teachers, people one step ahead of you are your chart's "Lu" — not the kind of help that hands you money, but the kind that hands you direction, opportunity, and one key sentence at the right moment. This help never announces itself in advance; you have to stay in touch for it to reach you.
  • Tian Liang transformed to Quan (Authority) in the Health Palace: Tian Liang is the shelter star; with authority added, your body and your state become things you govern — routine, rhythm, knowing when to push and when to fold. The more actively you manage them, the smoother the chart runs. Chronic late nights and brute endurance are this chart's most expensive way to live.
  • Zi Wei transformed to Ke (Fame) in the Life Palace: see Section 3. Reputation is your asset.
  • Tai Yin transformed to Ji (Friction) in the Siblings Palace: Tai Yin governs tenderness and care; under Ji, the misalignment "I thought they understood — they didn't" appears most easily among siblings, peers and equal-level partners. The fix is not in the chart, it is in one sentence: write important things down; do not rely on unspoken understanding. This is also the square the chart cannot let go of; the paid layer reads the Siblings and Friends palaces together in full. Here, we only mark it.

7. Career: with Lian Zhen, the arena matters more than the effort

For a Lian Zhen career chart, one thing is forbidden: fighting yourself in an arena that is not worth it. Your chart suits work with three shared features — the work is visible, you are judged on output, and reputation compounds: specialist, consultant, teacher, content-led founder all fit. A large hierarchy that promotes on seniority and patience with process runs against this chart.

Inside the current decade (Wealth Palace), the test for any opportunity becomes one sentence: can this become work, then reputation, then money? If yes, it is worth doing. If no, however lively it looks, it is a drain.

8. Wealth: in the Body Palace — late, but steady

Wealth Palace Tian Xiang, with the Body Palace in residence, draws a clear picture: no windfalls; reputation, monetised. In 2026 the annual Life Palace lands exactly on the Wealth Palace (Section 10) — a year for putting work out and being seen: fame first, money second; reverse the order and both jam.

Unknown: which specific months favour signing and which favour holding back requires laying out the twelve annual palaces. The sample layer does not write that; the paid layer gives it month by month.

9. Love: Tan Lang in the Spouse Palace wants someone alive

Tan Lang in the Spouse Palace: the partner you admire is vital, luminous, playful and serious at once. A Tan Lang relationship does not fear liveliness; it fears dullness — the moment a relationship becomes pure division of labour and process, it starts to leak air.

Tai Yin's friction in the Siblings Palace (Section 6) carries a reminder for close relationships too: you easily assume "they should understand," and stay silent while disappointed. The Zi Wei fix is the same as the BaZi one: agreements first — money, boundaries, the ugly words — written down, they make things last longer, not colder.

10. The current decade, and the 2026 annual cycle

Current decade cycle: the Wealth Palace (Tian Xiang + Body Palace). This is your decade's main axis — money and work. Fame first, monetisation after. A decade cycle does not promise any single year; it states the theme: turn reputation into an asset. Within this decade, every decision of the form "make the thing first, put it out" is one the chart supports; every "wait until it's safer" impulse is mostly Lian Zhen arguing with itself, not the chart blocking you.

2026 (Bing Wu year): the annual Life Palace lands precisely on your natal Wealth Palace — this is your year of being seen. Among Bing year's transformations, Lian Zhen turns to Ji (Friction) in your Career Palace: something at work will make you bristle this year — a partnership, an evaluation, a "why them and not me." That friction is not a bad sign; it is forcing you to choose your arena: fighting for something worthwhile is nerve; fighting for something worthless is self-consumption. Tian Tong turns to Lu in the Health Palace: this year's luck hides in your state — sleep well, keep the rhythm right, and opportunity knocks by itself; burn yourself out and even good luck cannot be caught.

One line only for 2027 (Ding Wei): Ju Men turns to Ji in the Children Palace — paperwork, words and contract details need unusual care. That is the year to price, sign and collect on what 2026 puts out — with the ugly words written first. The full year-by-year reading is in the paid layer.

11. Three themes that keep repeating

  1. Run your own table; don't climb by seniority. Life Palace Zi Wei–Tian Fu plus Travel Palace Qi Sha: your place is built by you, not granted.
  2. Reputation is an asset, not vanity. Zi Wei transformed to Ke in the Life Palace: protect your credit and the road widens.
  3. Money behind, work in front. Body Palace in Wealth with Tian Xiang presiding: fame first, income second — the order cannot be reversed.
