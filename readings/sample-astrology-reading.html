2. The big three: Sun, Moon, Rising

Sun in Virgo, 7th house — what you are here to be responsible for

The Sun is the main thread of your life. A Virgo Sun wants things done right and done usefully; placed in the 7th house, that thread runs through one-to-one relationships — a partner, a business partner, a key client. Working entirely on your own leaves you flat; being needed inside a relationship is what makes you feel lit up. One trap to watch: a 7th-house Sun easily treats "the other person is satisfied" as its own report card. Learn to tell apart which standards are yours, and which ones you are carrying on someone else's behalf.

Moon in Sagittarius, 9th house — your emotional way out

The Moon is who you are in private. A Sagittarius Moon cannot sit still; when feelings get stuck, it wants to move, to learn, to go and see somewhere new. In the 9th house, your sense of safety comes from "I am still learning, still moving forward." When life turns into repetition, you get restless first and low second — that is not ingratitude, it is your Moon asking to be fed. The fastest way to recharge your emotions is not rest; it is learning something new, or going somewhere you have never been.

Pisces Rising — the you that people meet first

The Ascendant is your front door. Pisces rising comes across as gentle, approachable, and unthreatening, and people pour their troubles out to you easily. But your Sun is Virgo — a soft front door with an exacting core. That contrast is your strongest social asset and your most tiring one: people assume you can absorb anything, while inside you keep a ruler, and everything that fails the measure gets quietly noted. You just don't say it out loud.

3. The planets, one by one

Mercury in Virgo, 7th house (conjunct the Sun) — a mind built for one-to-one conversation

Mercury in its own sign: precise thinking, a first-rate eye for detail. Sitting right beside your Sun in the 7th house, it points to your best working shape — deep one-to-one exchange: consulting, negotiation, writing proposals, bespoke services. Group chats and public speaking are not your home ground; one-to-one is. The catch: in conversation you tend to finish other people's sentences for them. Leave a little silence, and people will trust you more.

Venus in Leo, 6th house — needing to be seen at work

Venus rules what you love and how you love. A Leo Venus wants appreciation and a touch of stage; placed in the 6th house, the house of daily work, it hides a condition inside your job satisfaction: what I do has to be seen, and praised out loud. Quiet, unthanked work drains you; work with direct feedback feeds you. The same holds in love: you need affection stated plainly. Restrained, understated love does not reach you.

Mars in Scorpio, 8th house — your energy lives in the deep end

Mars is your drive. Scorpio Mars does not show off, but once it locks onto a target, its stamina is remarkable. In the 8th house — other people's resources, deep bonds — you are suited to handling other people's money, other people's secrets, and the complicated matters others won't touch: finance, investigation, crisis work, deep consulting are all your home ground. One caution: in intimacy, an 8th-house Mars can confuse control with care. When possessiveness rises, ask yourself one question first: am I protecting this, or am I making sure they can't leave?

Jupiter in Libra, 8th house — your benefactors sit inside other people's resources

Jupiter is your greatest source of luck. In Libra it works through partnership and fairness; in the 8th house, your good fortune usually arrives through someone else — a partner's resources, a spouse's support, a client's referral, an introduction somebody makes for you. On your own, you get by; drawing on others, you multiply. This point returns in the way-through section below.

Saturn in Aquarius, 11th house (retrograde) — the old lesson inside your circle

Saturn is your lifelong homework. In Aquarius in the 11th house, groups, circles, and communities always carry a faint sense of "I am the outsider here" — even after people have genuinely accepted you. Retrograde pulls it further inward: you hold belonging to a high standard, yet you will not reach out first. The homework runs in the opposite direction: do something genuinely useful for the circle first, and belonging grows by itself. You do not need to wait for others to claim you first.

Uranus and Neptune in Capricorn, 10th house (conjunct) — two giants living in your career house

A pairing your whole generation shares, sitting in the very centre of your career house: Uranus demands change, Neptune demands meaning, and both press on the 10th. Your working life was never going to be as simple as "find a stable job." What you do has to meet two conditions at once: a real-world foothold, and a meaning that reaches beyond the practical. Pure money work leaves you empty; pure ideal work leaves you hungry. Your answer sits where the two cross.

Pluto in Scorpio, 8th house — depth is your gift, and your examination hall

Pluto in its own sign, in the 8th house: you have a native radar for what lies deep in people — what others keep hidden, you sense at a glance. That is a gift, and it is also a burden: people who see too clearly are easily disappointed in others. Your homework is this: after seeing through someone, do not go cold. Stay willing to deal with people decently anyway.

4. Major aspects: where the chart pulls together, and where it pulls apart

  • Sun conjunct Mercury (0°): The Sun and Mercury sit together, so "who I am" and "how I think" are tightly fused — once you have thought something through, you act on it; and before you act, you have to think it through. The strength is inner consistency. The weakness: you find it hard to accept "I can't figure this out yet, but I'll take one step anyway."
  • Moon square Jupiter (about 5°): Moon in Sagittarius squaring Jupiter in Libra — when emotion rises, it wants to amplify: either all in, or all the way out. Learning to wait 24 hours at the peak of a feeling is a habit that will serve you for the rest of your life.
  • Mars conjunct Pluto (Scorpio, 8th house): Mars and Pluto together in the same sign and house — the strongest willpower configuration in your chart, and the one that most needs an outlet. With no clear target, this energy turns inward and becomes anxiety; with a target, it carries you through walls other people cannot get past.
  • Saturn trine Sun (about 4°): Saturn gives the Sun a solid floor — at your core you are someone who can carry weight, and the harder things get, the steadier you become. This is why people find you reliable: it is not an act, it is written into the chart.
  • Open question: Venus and Saturn form no exact aspect in this chart — so the question of whether giving and receiving balance out in love gets no fixed answer from the chart itself. It depends on the transits you are living through at the time.

5. Transits and timing: where you are right now

  • Saturn is transiting your 1st house (2026). This is a tightening season: the demands the outside world places on you rise, and your body and energy are warning you not to overdraw. It is not bad luck; it is an audit. Use this year to slowly clear out what no longer fits — the partnership that doesn't work, the relationship that drains you, the low-return job.
  • Jupiter enters your 5th house in mid-2026. From the second half of the year, chances to create, to be seen, and to fall in love increase noticeably. This is the best window in two years for putting your work out into the world. The preparation you do now is for that window.
  • Uranus is about to change signs. Over the next year or two, your career house will take an outside jolt — an industry shift, a new tool, a new form of collaboration. Keeping a side line ready in advance is worth ten times more than improvising when it lands.

6. Career, love, and money

Career

Your chart suits deep service, one-to-one: consulting, bespoke work, content, finance, and contract-type work. Assembly lines and pure execution suit you least. The breakthrough point sits in the 8th house: draw on other people's resources, and do the deep work others cannot do. The most worthwhile thing you can do right now: turn your service into a visible sample — like this reading — so people can see at a glance what you actually give.

Love

A 7th-house Sun plus Venus in Leo: you want to be taken seriously and loved out loud. Your ideal relationship is two people who stay independent, and still need each other. Watch one old pattern: in relationships you tend to give first and carry first, and only notice you are tired once the other person has fallen behind. The fix is simple — say what you need before you give, not after you have carried too much.

Money

An 8th-house cluster (Mars, Jupiter, Pluto): your money is tied to other people's money — dividends, commissions, revenue shares, client referrals. A flat salary keeps you going, but money truly grows through shares and word of mouth. Jupiter entering your 5th house in the second half of 2026 is the window for turning work into income.

7. What this chart does not say

  • The chart never says you are "destined" to be anything — it describes tendencies and timing. The same Saturn transit through the 1st house flattens one person, and helps another get lean and strong.
  • Whether giving and receiving balance out in love has no fixed answer (see section 4). That part depends on what you are living through, and on whether you are willing to speak first.
  • On health, a chart offers hints, never diagnoses: Venus in the 6th house plus Saturn transiting the 1st suggests keeping an eye on your sleep and your digestion. If your body is complaining, see a doctor — never take a chart as medical advice.

8. The way through: the next three months, concretely

  1. Make one visible sample. Write your service up as a finished piece like this reading, and put it in your shop and on your website — let buyers see your depth directly instead of guessing at it. It is your entry ticket to the Jupiter window in the second half of 2026.
  2. Cut one draining collaboration. In a year when Saturn crosses your 1st house, a low-return, high-drain relationship or partnership is a tax you keep paying for as long as you keep it. Cut one, and your energy comes back at once.
  3. Wait 24 hours when feelings peak. With the Moon square Jupiter, your most important decisions all deserve a night's sleep first. The habit itself is your talisman.
  4. Keep a side line. Uranus is about to knock on your career house: learn one new tool, gather one new audience, prepare one new form of service. The side line does not have to be big — it just has to exist.
